Result interpretation and input QC

Final candidates passed the standard GeneWise workflow, including the enabled family, copy and alignment-quality filters.

These computational candidates require biological review. High/medium/low grades describe sequence-disruption evidence, not the probability that a locus is a true pseudogene. Evidence grades and filtering decisions are recorded separately. GeneWise identity is amino-acid identity; miniprot identity is the PAF match proportion. These measurements are not directly equivalent. See evidence_status.tsv for assessed and unassessed evidence.
